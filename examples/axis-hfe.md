# Example: Axis-HFE

## Why Axis-HFE is related to TEO

**Axis-HFE** is a public hypothesis synthesis engine that explores multiple candidate ideas across a multidimensional evaluation field and includes a **Relational Layer** for mutual understanding, alignment, respect, and harmony.

TEO does not claim that HFE is a direct implementation of the ontology. Rather, HFE can be read as a resonant example of one of TEO's central intuitions:

> Meaningful cognition is not merely the selection of an isolated answer, but the structured interaction of multiple states within a relation-sensitive field.

In TEO terms, HFE is especially suggestive as an example related to **Shin / 心** — the dynamic state field in which interpretation, evaluation, and active synthesis unfold.

---

## Conceptual Mapping

| TEO concept | HFE-oriented reading |
|---|---|
| **Kon** | the design orientation or stable reasoning objective that preserves coherence |
| **Haku** | the executable engine, vector structure, constraints, and operational substrate |
| **Shin** | the evolving field of hypotheses, interpretations, scores, and syntheses |
| **Relation** | the Relational Layer that evaluates respect, alignment, and harmony alongside technical merit |

## Public Project Reference

- Repository: [Axis-HFE](https://github.com/Da-P-AIP/axis-hfe)

Axis-HFE publicly describes a reasoning loop that does not merely choose a statistically likely answer, but generates, evaluates, fuses, and self-corrects multiple hypotheses. Its Relational Layer makes it a useful public example of how cognition can be designed with relation in view rather than as isolated optimization alone.
